# Intrade Historical Archive v1.0.0

First documented research release of the historical snapshot added on 2 April 2013.

Includes original source preservation with checksums; contract-level coverage and diagnostics; source-traceable CSV/Parquet daily and trade tables; a searchable static explorer with per-contract original-file downloads; a tested loading example; data dictionary, citation and rights documentation.

Main summaries and supplemental closing-price histories remain distinct. Identical trade tuples and zero values are retained. Unquoted commas in source market names are repaired only by the documented fixed-schema rule and flagged. No settlement outcomes are inferred. The incomplete legacy datastore is preserved without claiming full recovery.

Read COVERAGE.md and quality_report.json before selecting a research sample. The DOI deposit is prepared but requires an authenticated preservation account and accurate rights metadata.
